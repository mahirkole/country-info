## Not included
- Mother's Day and Father's Day (first Sunday of May / June): listed in art. 123 but always Sundays; no extra day off.
- 13 January (Laisvės gynėjų diena): only found as a bill ("Projektas", planned effect 2026-01-01, https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/rs/legalact/TAP/a86e7ff0475d11f0a19dcea0bcc863ad/); could not confirm enactment, so not added. Caller should check.
- Caveat: I read the 2016 base text plus the 2019 amendment; I did not read the current consolidated version, so other later amendments to art. 123 cannot be ruled out.
