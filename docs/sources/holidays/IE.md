# IE evidence (VERIFIED). Source: Organisation of Working Time Act 1997, Second Schedule para 1. Revised Act (LRC, to Act 1/2025): https://revisedacts.lawreform.ie/eli/1997/act/20/revised/en/html ; enacted: https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/1997/act/20/schedule/2/enacted/en/html
- "Each of the following days shall, subject to the subsequent provisions of this Schedule, be a public holiday for the purposes of this Act: (a) Christmas Day, (b) St. Stephen's Day, (c) St. Patrick's Day,"
- "(d) Easter Monday, the first Monday in May, the first Monday in June and the first Monday in August,"
- "(e) the last Monday in October, (f) the 1st day of January, (g) any other day or days prescribed for the purposes of this paragraph."
## Not included
- Para 1(g) days prescribed by regulation (e.g. SI 419/2001 National Day of Mourning, one-off) and para 1/2 ministerial variation.
- St Brigid's Day (first Monday in February / 1 Feb): I know it is a public holiday since 2023 but did NOT find the establishing text in the revised Act (to Feb 2025 shows no mention of Brigid) nor the amending Act during this task; omitted rather than guessed.
- Good Friday: not a statutory public holiday in Ireland. Church holidays (6 Jan, Ascension, Corpus Christi etc., para 4) are employer-option substitutes only.
